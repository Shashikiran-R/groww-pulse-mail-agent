# Growg Weekly Pulse — 2026-W24  

**Executive Summary:** Trading execution glitches and high brokerage fees are the main pain points, while the UI continues to win praise from newcomers.

## 📊 Top Themes
### 1. Trading & Order Execution (39 reviews, 38.5% negative) →
Users report mismatched stop‑loss/target triggers and delayed or missing order fills, especially in options and F&O trades. Root causes stem from stale option‑chain data and inadequate validation of limit‑order parameters.

### 2. Usability & Positive Feedback (25 reviews, 20.0% negative) ↑
The majority of users love the clean, beginner‑friendly interface that simplifies market entry. Minor negative notes relate to occasional onboarding friction for first‑time investors.

### 3. Brokerage & Charges (6 reviews, 50.0% negative) 🆕
High brokerage fees are perceived as opaque and eroding trade profitability. Users feel the cost structure is not competitive compared with other discount brokers.

## 💡 Action Ideas
1. **[Trading & Order Execution]** Refactor the order‑routing engine to enforce real‑time price checks and add explicit confirmation dialogs for stop‑loss/target settings, reducing unintended executions.  
2. **[Usability & Positive Feedback]** Introduce an interactive onboarding tutorial that highlights key UI elements and common trade flows, preserving the current simplicity while easing first‑time user friction.  
3. **[Brokerage & Charges]** Publish a transparent fee matrix within the app and roll out a tiered brokerage model that aligns with market‑leader pricing, mitigating the perception of excessive charges.
