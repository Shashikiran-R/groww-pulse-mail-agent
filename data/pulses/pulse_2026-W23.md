# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026‑W23  

**Executive Summary:** Trading glitches and app lag dominate user pain points, while the UI continues to win praise from newcomers.

## 📊 Top Themes
### 1. Trading & Order Execution (52 reviews, 57.7% negative) →
Users report frequent mismatches in F&O data, delayed option‑chain updates, and stop‑loss/target triggers that fire incorrectly, leading to unexpected margin spikes. The root cause appears to be stale market feeds and insufficient validation of order‑execution logic.

### 2. App Performance / Bugs (19 reviews, 73.7% negative) 🆕
Slow loading times, frequent crashes after updates, and UI glitches disrupt real‑time trade monitoring. These issues stem from memory‑intensive rendering and inadequate regression testing on new OS versions.

### 3. Usability & Positive Feedback (31 reviews, 19.4% negative) ↓
Most users appreciate the clean, beginner‑friendly interface and find it easy to track stocks, with occasional suggestions for AI features. Positive sentiment is driven by intuitive navigation and clear visualizations.
