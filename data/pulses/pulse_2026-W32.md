# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026‑W32  

**Executive Summary:** Trading friction remains the biggest pain point, while the UI continues to win new users; high brokerage fees are driving sharp negative sentiment.

## 📊 Top Themes  

### 1. Trading & Order Execution (57 reviews, 66.7% negative) →  
Users struggle with F&O order failures, stale option‑chain data, and limited charting tools, leading to missed trades and frustration. The lack of a “clone app” feature compounds the inconvenience for multi‑account traders.  

### 2. Usability & Positive Feedback (53 reviews, 15.1% negative) ↑  
The clean, jargon‑free UI is repeatedly praised for making investing feel accessible to beginners. Positive sentiment is driven by ease of navigation and clear visual design, with only occasional minor complaints.  

### 3. Brokerage & Charges (13 reviews, 69.2% negative) 🆕  
High brokerage, AMC, and DP fees are perceived as uncompetitive, and users cite service delays and app instability during peak periods. This cost‑sensitivity is amplifying churn risk among price‑aware investors.

## 💡 Action Ideas  

1. **[Trading & Order Execution]** Implement a “clone session” mode that lets users maintain simultaneous logins across devices, reducing friction for multi‑broker workflows.  
2. **[Usability & Positive Feedback]** Expand the UI’s onboarding tutorials and add quick‑access help tips to preserve the low‑learning‑curve advantage for new investors.  
3. **[Brokerage & Charges]** Introduce a tiered brokerage model with discounted rates for high‑volume traders and transparently publish fee structures to rebuild trust.
