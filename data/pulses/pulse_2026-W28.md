# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026‑W28  

**Executive Summary:** Order execution glitches and support delays are eroding trust, even as the UI continues to win new users.

## 📊 Top Themes
### 1. Trading & Order Execution (39 reviews, 51.3% negative) →
Users report frequent failures when placing or exiting positions, especially in options and F&O, with stale option‑chain data and limited charting tools compounding frustration. The core cause is backend latency and incomplete market‑data integration.

### 2. Usability & Positive Feedback (29 reviews, 27.6% negative) →
The majority praise Groww’s clean design, intuitive navigation, and beginner‑friendly onboarding, driving high satisfaction and retention. Minor negative notes stem from occasional UI quirks on older devices.

### 3. Customer Support & Service (18 reviews, 83.3% negative) →
Support is perceived as unresponsive, with helpline calls unanswered and tickets taking days to resolve, leaving users feeling abandoned during critical issues. The lack of live agents amplifies the perception of neglect.
