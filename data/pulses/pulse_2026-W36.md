# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026-W36  

**Executive Summary:** Trading glitches and support delays dominate user frustration, while UI stability remains a critical lever for retention.

## 📊 Top Themes  

### 1. Trading & Order Execution (58 reviews, 60.3% negative) →  
Users report sluggish chart loading, inconsistent stop‑loss/target triggers, and frequent F&O order failures, often tied to a clunky interface that forces extra navigation. The underlying cause appears to be outdated permission handling and sub‑optimal integration of TradingView components.

### 2. Usability & Positive Feedback (41 reviews, 22.0% negative) ↑  
Most reviewers praise the clean UI and beginner‑friendly onboarding, yet a vocal minority flags disruptive UI redesigns that alter button placements and lead to accidental trades. The root issue is a lack of UI change communication and insufficient user testing for high‑frequency traders.

### 3. Customer Support & Service (16 reviews, 68.8% negative) 🆕  
Support tickets linger for up to a week, with helpline agents often unresponsive, leaving users without timely resolutions for even minor problems. This stems from understaffed support channels and an opaque escalation workflow.

## 💡 Action Ideas  

1. **[Trading & Order Execution]** Refactor permission requests to follow Android 13+ guidelines and isolate TradingView loading to a background thread, reducing latency and privacy concerns.  
2. **[Usability & Positive Feedback]** Institute a UI change log with in‑app walkthroughs for any layout alteration, and lock critical trade‑execution buttons behind a confirmation step to prevent accidental orders.  
3. **[Customer Support & Service]** Deploy a tiered ticketing system with SLA dashboards, expand helpline staffing during peak hours, and introduce an AI‑assisted FAQ bot to triage low‑complexity issues.
