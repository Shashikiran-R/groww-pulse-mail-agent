## 📊 Top Themes  

### 1. Usability & Positive Feedback (31 reviews, 19.4% negative) ↑  
Users laud the clean, beginner‑friendly UI and overall satisfaction, but occasional minor friction points still surface. The high positive ratio indicates the core experience is solid, yet there’s room to tighten edge‑case flows.

### 3. Customer Support & Service (18 reviews, 88.9% negative) →  
Helpline silence, slow ticket handling, and inaccurate IPO subscription figures dominate negative sentiment, painting the support function as a major pain point. The stagnant trend underscores the urgency of a service overhaul.
