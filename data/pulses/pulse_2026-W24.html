📊 Top Themes

1. Trading & Order Execution (39 reviews, 38.5% negative) →

Users report mismatched stop‑loss/target triggers and delayed or missing order fills, especially in options and F&O trades. Root causes stem from stale option‑chain data and inadequate validation of limit‑order parameters.

2. Usability & Positive Feedback (25 reviews, 20.0% negative) ↑

The majority of users love the clean, beginner‑friendly interface that simplifies market entry. Minor negative notes relate to occasional onboarding friction for first‑time investors.

3. Brokerage & Charges (6 reviews, 50.0% negative) 🆕

High brokerage fees are perceived as opaque and eroding trade profitability. Users feel the cost structure is not competitive compared with other discount brokers.
