# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026‑W35  

**Executive Summary:** Trading execution friction and recent update bugs are driving negative sentiment, while the app’s core usability continues to earn strong praise.

## 📊 Top Themes
### 1. Trading & Order Execution (85 reviews, 57.6% negative) →
Users report broken indicators, unreliable F&O order fills, and stop‑loss/target triggers that fail to fire, limiting deeper market research. The lack of advanced charting tools forces experienced traders to look elsewhere.

### 2. App Performance / Bugs (25 reviews, 60.0% negative) →
The latest app update introduced UI glitches—most notably a mis‑positioned scalper button—and slower load times, eroding confidence in day‑to‑day usage. Bug regression testing appears insufficient for rapid release cycles.

### 3. Usability & Positive Feedback (53 reviews, 18.9% negative) →
New and seasoned investors appreciate Groww’s clean interface, easy‑to‑use investment flows, and portfolio tracking, reinforcing its positioning as a beginner‑friendly platform. Positive sentiment outweighs the few usability complaints.
