Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026-W39

Executive Summary: Support responsiveness and order‑execution reliability are the biggest pain points this week, while the UI continues to win new users.

📊 Top Themes

1. Customer Support & Service (19 reviews, 78.9% negative) 🆕

Users repeatedly report that the helpline never answers and tickets sit unresolved for days, creating frustration and churn risk. The lack of a live human agent compounds the perception of a “black‑box” support system.

2. Trading & Order Execution (49 reviews, 63.3% negative) 🆕

Stop‑loss and target triggers frequently fail, and option‑chain data is inaccurate, leading to missed trades and losses. Combined with general lag and F&O glitches, the platform feels unreliable for active traders.

3. Usability & Positive Feedback (43 reviews, 32.6% negative) 🆕

The clean UI, intuitive navigation, and smooth onboarding are repeatedly praised, driving high satisfaction among beginners. Positive sentiment outweighs minor usability complaints, confirming the design’s market fit.
