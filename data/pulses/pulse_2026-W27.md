# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026-W27  

**Executive Summary:** Trading glitches are driving negative sentiment, while UI praise remains strong and support experiences are mixed.

## 📊 Top Themes
### 1. Trading & Order Execution (45 reviews, 53.3% negative) ↑  
Users report frequent mismatches in F&O calculations, stale option‑chain data, and unreliable stop‑loss/target triggers, leading to loss of confidence in trade accuracy. The root cause appears to be delayed market data feeds and insufficient validation in order‑execution logic.

### 2. Usability & Positive Feedback (55 reviews, 14.5% negative) ↓  
The clean interface, beginner‑friendly flows, and smooth trading features earn high marks, driving strong advocacy. Minor negative notes stem from occasional onboarding hiccups, but overall satisfaction is rising.

### 3. Customer Support & Service (6 reviews, 50.0% negative) 🆕  
Half of the recent tickets cite unresponsive helpline and slow resolution, while a few users still commend the ticket system. Inconsistency in support staffing and SLA enforcement is the likely driver.
