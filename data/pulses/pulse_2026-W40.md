# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026‑W40  

**Executive Summary:** Trading functionality gaps and deteriorating support are the biggest pain points, while the UI continues to earn praise.

## 📊 Top Themes  

### 1. Trading & Order Execution (31 reviews, 45.2% negative) ↑  
Users repeatedly flag missing GTT stop‑loss/target, lack of trailing stop‑loss, and absent OI data on the one‑tap screen. F&O and option‑chain inconsistencies further erode confidence in order placement.

### 2. Usability & Positive Feedback (25 reviews, 24.0% negative) ↑  
The majority applaud the clean design and intuitive navigation, but a vocal minority finds the onboarding flow confusing and the mandatory bank‑link step intrusive. Frustration spikes when first‑time users cannot explore the interface before committing funds.

### 3. Customer Support & Service (8 reviews, 75.0% negative) ↓  
Helpline silence and prolonged ticket resolution dominate complaints, especially around nominee documentation. Repeated ticket closures without fixes amplify user dissatisfaction.

## 💡 Action Ideas  

1. **[Trading & Order Execution]** Implement GTT stop‑loss/target and trailing stop‑loss on the one‑tap screen within the next sprint; expose real‑time OI data for options to close the feature gap.
