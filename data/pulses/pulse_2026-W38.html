Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026‑W38

Executive Summary: Trading glitches and support gaps are driving a surge in negative sentiment, while the app’s clean UI continues to win new users.

📊 Top Themes

1. Trading & Order Execution (51 reviews, 56.9% negative) ↑

Users report delayed real‑time price feeds, frequent option‑chain mismatches, and stop‑loss/target orders that never trigger. The root cause is an unstable market‑data pipeline and insufficient validation in the F&O execution engine.

2. Customer Support & Service (20 reviews, 85.0% negative) ↑

Helpline calls go unanswered and there is a lack of live agents, leaving users stranded with critical account issues. This stems from understaffed support tiers and an over‑reliance on automated responses.

3. Usability & Positive Feedback (41 reviews, 24.4% negative) ↓

Most reviewers praise the intuitive UI, beginner‑friendly flows, and clean navigation, though a minority still cite occasional glitches. The positive perception is anchored in consistent design language and onboarding tutorials.
