# Groww Weekly Pulse — 2026‑W34  

**Executive Summary:** Trading reliability and app stability are the biggest pain points, while the clean UI continues to win users’ hearts.  

## 📊 Top Themes  

### 1. Trading & Order Execution (40 reviews, 52.5% negative) ↑  
Users report frequent failures of stop‑loss and target‑price orders, especially during high‑volume intraday trades, and cite hidden charges that erode confidence. The root cause appears to be fragile order‑routing logic and insufficient fee transparency in the interface.  

### 2. App Performance / Bugs (17 reviews, 58.8% negative) 🆕  
Slow loading screens, occasional login hiccups, and regressions after updates dominate complaints, indicating performance regressions in recent releases. Users also request richer portfolio metrics, showing a gap between expectations and current data offerings.  

### 3. Usability & Positive Feedback (26 reviews, 26.9% negative) ↓  
The majority praise Groww’s intuitive UI, clean design, and easy navigation, reinforcing the app’s core strength. Negative notes are limited to minor usability quirks, suggesting the experience is largely meeting user expectations.

## 💡 Action Ideas  

1. **[Trading & Order Execution]** Refine order‑routing and stop‑loss execution engines to handle peak volumes, and add a clear, upfront fee breakdown in the trade screen.  
2. **[App Performance / Bugs]** Optimize API latency and implement lazy loading for heavy screens; introduce a resilient login retry mechanism and monitor post‑update performance metrics.  
3. **[Usability & Positive Feedback]** Expand the praised UI with contextual onboarding tips for new investors and ensure design consistency as new features (e.g., advanced charts) are rolled out.
